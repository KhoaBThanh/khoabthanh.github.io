You only live once, but if you do it right, once is enough

January 14, 2021 · Story

There are many ways to live in this world, and it is up to each person to choose how to live. We cannot predict the future; all that matters is what is happening right now. Do not be too worried about your choice, because whether it produces the result you expect will be decided by your effort.

In life, there are many opportunities. There is no such thing as a good or a wrong choice, because each option leads to a different set of options. The path you are on is the consequence of a great many decisions.

When you take responsibility for your acts, and face the consequences of your choices, you are mature. Always be willing to admit and to correct your mistakes. Remember that you own your life, and that the only way for each of us to find our own worth is to live it. Let no one direct your life; follow where your heart leads instead.

Life is not as easy and as bright as it looks in films and novels. Real life is a mixture of light and dark. Many obstacles stand on the road to the top, and they serve to discourage us. Do not give up too quickly, because the road to success is paved with flowers and with hurdles that you must overcome. However bad things become, stay strong. Behind those difficulties, God has prepared a gift for us.
