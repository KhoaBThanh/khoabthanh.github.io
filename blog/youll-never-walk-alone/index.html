You'll never walk alone

January 12, 2021 · Story

This world is filled with intrinsic risks, including the potential perils of insecurity. People feel thrilled and inspired when they listen to “You’ll Never Walk Alone”. When you are going through a storm, keep your head up and do not be afraid of the dark night ahead. After the storm there is a bright sky and the sweet silver song of a lark. Walk on through the wind, through the rain, through the hopes that have been thrown away. Walk on with hope in your heart, and you will never walk alone.

When you walk through a storm
Hold your head up high
And don’t be afraid of the dark
At the end of a storm
There’s a golden sky

The words are by Oscar Hammerstein II, with music by Richard Rodgers, for the musical Carousel. The best known recording is by Gerry and the Pacemakers. Listen to it on YouTube.
