I am pleased to share that my researcher profile appears in the Stanford Top
2% Scientists single-year records listed by TopSciNet. The profile identifies
my affiliation with the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City and connects
my work with Information and Communication Technologies, Artificial
Intelligence and Image Processing, and Business and Management.

The Top 2% list uses citation-based indicators to identify researchers whose
work has received notable scholarly attention within their fields. These
indicators offer one way to understand research influence, but they cannot
capture every dimension of scholarship, including teaching, practical impact,
or the contributions of collaborators.

The profile's latest visible single-year record is for 2025. It reports a rank
of 4,127 among 458,615 researchers in Artificial Intelligence and Image
Processing, with self-citations excluded. This is a 2025 figure and should not
be confused with the 2019 year in the profile link.

I am grateful to the colleagues, students, and research partners whose ideas
and collaboration have shaped this work. Recognition is an encouragement to
keep asking useful questions and to pursue research that contributes to both
scholarship and practice.
