Global top percentile is the statistical percentile reported for the full
eligible population in that topic and period, not just the published Top
1000. Vietnam rank is shown as rank / number of Vietnam-based scholars in that
topic's published World Top1000; Vietnam top percentile is calculated from
that national rank and denominator. It therefore describes position within
that listed group, not among every researcher in Vietnam.

I am grateful to the colleagues, students, and research partners whose
contributions have shaped this work. These indicators are one snapshot of
scholarly activity, not its full measure. I hope this recognition will
encourage me to continue pursuing research that is rigorous, collaborative,
and useful to the communities it serves.
