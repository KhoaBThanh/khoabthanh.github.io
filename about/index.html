About

Bui Thanh Khoa (Bùi Thành Khoa) is a lecturer at the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City, where he has taught in the Faculty of Commerce and Tourism since 2009. He holds a PhD in Business Administration from Ho Chi Minh City Open University (2020) and a master’s degree in Business and Economics from Universite Toulouse 1 Capitole in France (2012).

He is the co-founder and CEO of Innoteq Co., Ltd., a technology company in Ho Chi Minh City. He serves on the editorial boards of several international indexed journals as well as Journal of Science and Technology - Ho Chi Minh City University of Industry (Vietnam).

His research covers electronic commerce, organisational behaviour, and consumer behaviour, with attention to intention, satisfaction, and loyalty. He also reviews for journals indexed in Scopus and ISI.
